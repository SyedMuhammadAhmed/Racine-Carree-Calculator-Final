About Racine Carrée

Racine Carrée (French for "Square Root") is a premium, open-source calculator suite designed for students, engineers, scientists, and anyone who works with numbers.

Built with modern web technologies, it delivers instant, precise results with a beautiful interface that makes math feel effortless. Every pixel, animation, and interaction has been carefully crafted for the best possible experience.
